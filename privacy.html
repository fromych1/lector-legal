Lector: Audiobook Reader - Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Lector ("the app") is an e-reader and audiobook player that converts text to speech entirely on your device. This policy explains what data the app handles.

1. Your books stay on your device

Books you import (EPUB, FB2, PDF, TXT, ZIP), their text, reading progress, bookmarks and quotes are stored locally on your device. Book text is never uploaded to our servers or to any third-party speech service. Neural voice synthesis runs offline on your device.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, or the content of your books.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing processes subscriptions and purchases. We receive only purchase status (whether PRO is active); payment details are handled by Google.
  • Google AdMob shows rewarded video ads in the free tier. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address and device information to serve and measure ads. In the EEA, UK and other regions, we ask for your consent through Google's consent form (UMP) and you can change your choice at any time. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Downloads. When you download a free catalog book or a neural voice model, your device requests the file from a public host (e.g. Project Gutenberg, GitHub or a model host). Those hosts may see your IP address.

4. Permissions

  • Notifications - media playback controls on the lock screen (requested when you start playback).
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock - to keep audio playing with the screen off.
  • Internet - catalog and voice downloads, billing and ads.

5. Data retention and deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Use Settings в†’ Clear audio cache to remove generated audio.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal information.

7. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change accordingly.
